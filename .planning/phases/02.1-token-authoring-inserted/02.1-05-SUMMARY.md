## The instrument, and what it caught

The checks were developed against a scratch copy of the shipped harness with the candidate block spliced in above its own summary line and `HTML_PATH` repointed at the worktree artifact. The probe and the gate are therefore **literally the same file**, which is the strongest form of wave 3's rule that a row must not be green in the probe and red in the gate.

It earned its keep immediately. Checks 36-38 read **green-adjacent nonsense** on the first run — `cut to 30 code points`, `loud panel=false` — because the **stub's `#tok-pick-name` node carried no class**. `[S07.2]` tells the name field apart by its class, exactly as `[S07.1]` tells a stepper field apart by its own, so every keystroke, Enter, Escape and blur handler was declining the event on its first line. Three checks driving a field nothing was listening to. The stub now carries the class and the comment beside it says why it is not decoration.

A second finding, one layer up: after check 36 the field still held focus, and the repaint **skips a focused field on purpose** (D-19). A rename raised from outside to reset the fixture therefore left both the text and the recorded baseline standing — correct behaviour, and a trap for the next case. `nameLeave()` exists for that and says so.

## What the Node gate cannot cover — for plan 02.1-06's rehearsal
