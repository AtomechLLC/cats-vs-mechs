Three constraints decide the whole layout and every one of them is written down somewhere in the file
already.

**One. The topbar reservation is exactly two controls and this phase spends both.** `:1057-1085` says
so in its own words: *"The cluster's remaining reservation is Phase 5's turn state and start-fight,
and after those two the reservation is spent. A later phase wanting a control per action, per side or
per round gets the answer D-05 already gave: put it in the surface, not on the bar."* Read literally,
Phase 5 gets **one** start-fight control and **one** readout. The declaration surface, Advance, the
fight reset and the ledger are *"the surface, not the bar"*, and the todo's *"a simple input at the
top"* means at the top of the **page**, not in the bar cluster.

**Two. The ledger must not live inside `#board`.** `structure()` calls `withPreservedFocus(board, …)`
and `keyed()` takes the **first** `[data-k]` match scoped to `#board`. Measured: five cloned boards
inside `#board` above the columns make the first match a **dead ledger node**, so focus restore after
any structural rebuild lands on a clone. Outside `#board`, the first match is live.

**Three. There is no append-only list render anywhere in this artifact.** Every list surface is
`replaceChildren()` and rebuild, or build-once-write-every-frame. The one structure in the file that
only grows is the undo stack, and it is capped at 30 and never rendered. So the ledger's home, its
growth direction and its scroll behaviour are all decisions with no precedent, and they get made here,
in the register `clampTokenName` and `#reset-ask` use for a technique with no analog.

Purpose: the roots, the styles and the gate's knowledge of them, arriving together as the file's
three-part rule requires. Output: two regions, `[C14]`, and a bidirectionally gated id group.
