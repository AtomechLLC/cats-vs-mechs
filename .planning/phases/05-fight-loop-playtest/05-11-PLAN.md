    1. **D-26 — how does a fight end?** The assumption taken was: **it does not.** A fight stops when

       thing you can *read*, not a thing the tool *says*.

       It was inferred from a *prohibition* — the no-verdict rule — and a prohibition tells you what
       not to build, not what to build. It is entirely possible you want *something* to mark the
       moment: a neutral statement of fact, a prompt to reset, a ledger entry. "Nothing at all" may
       read as the tool losing interest rather than as the tool declining to judge.

       **So play until one side is gone, and then tell us what that felt like.** Did it land, or did
       it fall flat? Adding a neutral end-of-fight reading later is additive and cheap; adding one
