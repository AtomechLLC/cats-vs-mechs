<objective>
**The battle, on the fight tab.**

D-27's addendum, verbatim:

> I want a visual presentation of the battle field on the current turn (shapes for cats with smaller
> shapes for status points / health on them- on one side, same on the right for the other side)

This is the piece that puts unit state back on the fight tab. The earlier plan set recorded a
consequence of the tab — *"the unit cards are on the board tab, so a student switches views to see
health"* — and the developer answered it with a surface rather than accepting it. **Hand rulings
stay on the board tab.** The battlefield is a reading, plus one job: it is where targets light.

**It reuses the board's own token machinery and re-derives nothing.** `[S06]` is one function scope,
so `syncRow`, `makeToken`, `styleFor`, `labelFor` and `COMPACT_AT` are directly callable — and
calling them is the requirement, not a convenience. It is what makes a student-authored token type
appear in the battle exactly as they authored it (D-07's "they are the same kind of thing", ALLOC-10,
D-24's no-second-tier), it is what keeps `--tok`'s rehearsal dial meaning one thing, and it is what
brings Rules 1, 2 and 3 along: grow and shrink by DELTA only, never re-append, never reorder — because
*"every token replaying its pop on a game-feel course artifact is a correctness bug, not a polish
item."*

**FIGHT-04 and FIGHT-06 are read here too.** *"Dead units stay visible in the roster rather than
disappearing"* — the battlefield is the second place that promise now has to hold, and it is the
place a student is actually looking during a fight.

Purpose: the battle you can see.
Output: `[S06.11]`, `[C14.4]`, and the rows that read them off the page.
