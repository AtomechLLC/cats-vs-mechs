<the_line_this_phase_walks>
A hand ruling is the **student** deciding, so these ops must be maximally permissive about what a
student may decide and maximally strict about what the tool may infer.

- `setAlive` writes the flag and **nothing else**. It does not set health. A unit ruled dead at full
  health and a unit ruled alive at zero are both representable and both are the point.
- The health and shield ops write their own number and **nothing else**. Neither writes `alive`.
- Nothing here reads `REFERENCE.beats` and nothing writes `keywords`.
- Nothing here refuses a value on the grounds that it is implausible. A student ruling a unit to 40
  health because their Recharge house rule says so is the workshop working correctly; the only bounds
  are `int()`'s existing type-and-range contract, which exists so a `null` or a `'7'` fails loudly
  rather than laundering into 0 mid-fight.
- Nothing here decides the fight is over (D-26).

The record these ops write is a **fact about what the student did**, not a judgement about it. It
says a number went from one value to another, by hand, in this round. It does not say why, and it does
not say whether it was right.
</the_line_this_phase_walks>
