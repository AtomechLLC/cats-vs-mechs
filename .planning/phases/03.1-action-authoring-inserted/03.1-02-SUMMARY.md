## Decisions Made

1. **`DAMAGE_KEYS` ships health-only,** and the whole reading lives in its comment: the D-05c shield rule stated plainly against the reading it is not, why the scope is nonetheless health-only, both measured outcomes, and the correction to D-14a.
2. **`turnsToWipe` calls `bestPair` once.** Reading the hit separately would let a run with `activeUnits` supplied pick one action for the per-turn figure and another for the hit, and `[S06.3]` prints both side by side as if they described the same swing. See Deviation 1.
3. **CONTEXT D-14a is corrected by measurement.** It says the shipped board should read about 5 rather than about 9 and instructs the planner to expect the strip to move. Measured under both readings in the same run: it does not move, because no shipped action carries a shield transformation and the pool `factionEhp` computes already holds both halves. The plan anticipated this; probe G is the evidence.
4. **The new `[S09.8]` rows read detached `defaults()` copies.** No op authors an action until plan 03.1-03, and reaching into `[S05]` from here would touch a region this plan does not own. One drive through a real shipped op (`setFactionAp`) is bracketed with `App.state.restore` at the end, so the "reads its argument and never the singleton" claim is held by more than a comment.
5. **`affordability` hands back `{ apCost, apHave, met: [{ tok, need, have }] }` and nothing else.** The rule that binds every consumer — this report may never disable a control — is written in the function's own comment, with the picker's Remove button named as the contrast.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 — Bug] `turnsToWipe` would have read its hit from a different action than its per-turn figure**

- **Found during:** Task 1.
- **Issue:** `turnsToWipe` called `factionDps(attacker, activeUnits)` and then `bestDamage(attacker)` — the second without forwarding `activeUnits`. Rewiring both onto `bestPair` without touching `turnsToWipe` would leave a run with a live fight's survivor count picking one action for `perTurn` and another for `hit`. `soakTotal` then divides by that hit, and `[S06.3]` prints both operands on a projector as though they described one swing. Nothing in Phase 3 supplies `activeUnits`, so this would have shipped silent until Phase 5.
- **Fix:** `turnsToWipe` takes one `bestPair` call and reads both fields off it, with the reason written above the function.
- **Committed in:** `ee7ad98`

**2. [Rule 1 — Bug] A `[S09.8]` fixture became stale the moment damage was read from the transformation list**

- **Found during:** Task 1, caught by the harness.
- **Issue:** `nor does a side whose actions all do nothing to health` builds its fixture with `a.dmg = 0` and nothing else. Since plan 03.1-01, Lasers carries `xf: [{ who: 'target', tok: 'hp', d: -3 }]`, and `actionDamage` reads the list in preference to the scalar — so the fixture described a side that still hits for three, and the row went red with `{"perTurn":9,...}` against an expected `null`.
- **Fix:** the fixture clears both fields, with a comment saying why the shorter spelling is no longer that board. A second row was added asserting the half the old spelling missed: clearing only the scalar leaves `bestDamage` at 3.
- **Committed in:** `ee7ad98`

**3. [Rule 3 — Blocking] Three row labels tripped Layer B of the PROJ-06 gate**

- **Found during:** Task 3.
- **Issue:** `does not win against`, `wins on throughput` and `is better than assuming` are the natural English for "this action is the one the derivation reads", and all three are on the 23-word literal list:
  ```
  PROJ-06 VIOLATION: a comparative word reached a rendered string, not a comment (3):
    [win]:    'a two-point action taking seven health does not win against a '
    [wins]:   'a three-point action taking twelve health wins on throughput and '
    [better]: 'no idea what it is worth and saying so is better than assuming'
  ```
- **Fix:** all three reworded to describe the mechanism rather than a contest — *becomes the pair the projection reads*, *falls short of*, *is what D-16 asks for instead of assuming a number*. Worth recording as a hazard for plans 03.1-04 and 03.1-06: the phase's subject matter makes "wins" the first word that comes to hand, and a self-test label is a string literal like any other.
- **Committed in:** `c0417ad`

**4. [Rule 1 — Bug] `actionModelled` carried a second arm that reproduced the first, making probe H vacuous**

- **Found during:** Task 3, by running probe H and getting green.
- **Issue and fix:** see PROBE H above.
- **Committed in:** `c0417ad`
