<objective>
Show the student their own rule, let them change it, and land nothing.

This is the one work unit in the phase with no analog anywhere in the file. Nothing in
`cats-vs-mechs.html` today stages a change for approval: swatches dispatch on the press, fields
commit on Enter or blur, and Remove commits with no confirmation and says so on purpose. The file
has a precedent for how to RECORD a technique with no precedent — `clampTokenName` opens by saying
it is the one technique in its phase with none, and then explains itself at length. Do that here.

The proposal is not state. All four homes were measured: a `ui` key reddens a row and the `ui`
writer refuses object values by contract; a fourth slice reddens another; nesting under the build
slice reddens nothing but is the wrong home, because the build slice is the student's allocation and
a proposal has not happened. It is a FORM on a dialog. That is the mechanical expression of
"nothing lands until they say so": there is no state in which a proposal exists and has not been
accepted, so nothing downstream — undo, the build code, the projection — can ever read one.

Purpose: ACT-05's first half, ACT-06 and ACT-07.
Output: a pane that restates a rule, an affordability report that rules on nothing, a refusal by
name, and two checks proving nothing lands.
</objective>
