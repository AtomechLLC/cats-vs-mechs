    14. **The damage-key scope for the projection.** Damage spends shield before health, point for
        point — that is your rule (D-05c) and it is implemented. The open question is what the
        PROJECTION counts as damage. It ships counting health only, so an action that strips shield
        is NAMED in the admission line rather than counted. The alternative counts shield too.
        Measured, both ways, on this board:
          - health only: the shield-stripping action reads about 9 turns, plus
            `Not shown in these figures: <action>.`
          - health and shield: it reads about 5 turns, with nothing admitted.
        Health-only was chosen because a shield pool is bounded at 3 per Mech while the projection
        assumes the action repeats without limit, so counting it reports more throughput than the
        board can deliver — and overstating is the one direction the no-verdict rule forbids.
        **Also, a correction worth a sentence:** the phase context said the SHIPPED board would move
        from about 9 to about 5 and that fixtures should be updated. Measured, it does not move
        either way — no shipped action carries a shield transformation, and the pool the projection
        already used includes shield. Nothing moved, and the plans pin that.
        **Confirm health-only, or ask for health and shield.**

        now** — which would require rewriting that Out of Scope entry, the thing you declined once.
