<objective>
Turn, deliberately, the one shipped assertion this phase was always going to redden — and give the
page three things it can now say.

`refActions()` reads the FROZEN board, and its own comment predicted this day in writing: *the day
actions become student-editable, this read is the line that has to change on purpose.* Today is
that day. Measured consequence of not doing it: an authored action is live in state and invisible on
the page.

Two second-order effects come with it, and both are in this plan rather than left to be discovered.
Once a card's text is student-supplied, a student who names an action after a comparative word would
redden Layer C on a legitimate build — so the card needs an exemption channel of its own. And once
the projection can refuse to model an action, it must say which ones, plainly, rather than quietly
omitting half the board.

Purpose: the page reads the student's rules, and admits what it cannot read.
Output: cards on the build slice, an admission line, and a warning before a removal breaks a rule.
</objective>
