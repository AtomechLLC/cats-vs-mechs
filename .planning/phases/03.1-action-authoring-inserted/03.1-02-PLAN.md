<objective>
Decide what the projection MEANS once actions are programmable, and implement that decision as
three pure reads plus one report.

`bestDamage` is `max(action.dmg)` — one scalar per faction — and Phase 3's whole projection rests
on it through `factionDps` -> `turnsToWipe` -> the strip. An action that costs two points, heals
its caster, or moves a student-made token does not reduce to that. It generalises into
`max over actions of min(floor(ap / apCost), active) x damage`, which reproduces the shipped board
exactly, gets LESS confident under an expensive big hit rather than more, and names what it cannot
model.

Purpose: D-14 answered with the numbers behind it, and D-15's rule kept — saying less is allowed,
implying a verdict is not.
Output: `[S02]` reads that later plans render, and rows that pin the shipped figures unchanged.
</objective>

    one point. A cost list of any length other than one, a cost naming a token other than action

    `actionDamage(a)` returns per-use damage: the magnitude of the action's negative deltas on the

    `a.dmg`, which keeps the shipped six exactly as they read today.

    and never to the name — a student renaming Health to Vigor changes `name` and nothing else.
    Beside it, write the reading down, because the next reader will otherwise "fix" it:

      - The shield reading this project uses (D-05c): damage applies to the target's shield first,
        POINT FOR POINT, then overflows to health. Three damage against three shield and six health
        leaves shield zero and health six. This is not the alternative reading in which one hit
        consumes exactly one shield point and then lands in full.
      - Why the damage-key scope is nonetheless health only: a shield pool is bounded at three per
        Mech, while this derivation assumes the action repeats without limit. Counting a shield
        strip would report more throughput than the board can deliver — the one direction PROJ-06
        forbids. Under health-only the action is not dropped, it is named (Task 2).

        shipped action carries a shield transformation. The pool `factionEhp` computes already
        includes shield.

    `bestPair(faction, activeUnits)` returns `{ hit, perTurn }`: over every action whose
    `actionApCost` is not `null`, the number of uses is `min(floor(ap / cost), active)`, the product
