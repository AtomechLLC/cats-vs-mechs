<objective>
Prove the refusal, one guard at a time, and build the tripwire for the failure nobody will be
looking for.

Plan 04-02 proved the codec works. This plan proves it refuses — which is the half SHARE-03 is
actually about, and the half most likely to be green about nothing. Research measured all thirteen
tamper shapes as refused, and every one of them **recomputed the checksum first**, so it reached the
content guards rather than stopping at the digest. A refusal suite that skips that step is a suite
asserting the checksum seventeen times.

And the one failure this phase can see coming but cannot prevent: **`action.dmg` and
`action.keywords` are not encoded**, because no `[S05]` op writes either and both are therefore
reconstructible — shipped actions from `DEFAULTS`, authored ones from `createAction`'s constants. The
day a later phase ships a `setActionDmg`, every shared build silently loses it, in a codec nobody
thought to revisit, and nothing anywhere would say so. Research specified two cheap mitigations and
asked the plan to take at least the first. **This plan takes both.**

Purpose: SHARE-03's refusal half, proved guard by guard, plus the assumption log's A3 turned into a
mechanism.
Output: the hostile half of `[S09.11]`, one new gate check, and any guard the probes showed missing.
</objective>
